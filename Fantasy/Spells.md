# Magic and Spells

All magic comes from [[The Others|the Others]], ancient beings once worshipped as gods. Some resemble fey sovereigns, living elements, old ones or chaos gods, but these are aspects rather than separate kinds of magic. A spell is a **Magical Ability** conveyed through a pact with one of these beings.

## Pacts

When a character learns their first Magical Ability, define the pact that grants it. Establishing the pact does not cost an additional Ability. Record four details:

- **The Other:** Its name or title.
- **Aspects:** Two or three things within its influence, such as beasts, fire, dreams, decay, storms or transformation.
- **Sign:** The unnatural trace that accompanies its magic.
- **Demand:** The service, offering or behaviour it habitually expects.

Later spells must broadly fit the Other's Aspects or be granted through events in play. A character can enter a second pact, but this should be a consequential event rather than an ordinary advancement choice.

## Learning spells

Spells are learned exactly like other Abilities. The lists below describe normal access:

- **Witches** bargain directly with the Others.
- **Alchemists** bind their influence into substances, diagrams and prepared vessels.
- **Vicars** invoke them through doctrine and recognised rites, sometimes without understanding the true identity of the power answering them.

A character from another Profession can learn a spell after gaining access to a pact through play.

## Casting spells

A cost written as **1–3 Focus** means that the caster must spend at least 1 Focus and may spend up to 3. The caster chooses the amount before rolling.

If a spell calls for a test, make it when the spell is activated. The Profession benefit does not make a casting or magical attack test easier unless the spell says otherwise.

Roll one **Price Die** for every Focus spent:

- Each **1** adds one **Complication level**.
- Each **10+** adds one **Power level**.
- Other results have no effect.

Power and Complication do not cancel each other. Price Dice never generate Focus and cannot normally be modified or rerolled. If a casting test fails, the spell has no effect and rolled Power is lost, but any Complication still occurs.

Unless an option is marked **repeatable**, each listed Power option can be chosen only once per casting.

### Complications

The GM describes a consequence appropriate to the Other, the spell and the situation:

| Level              | Consequence                                                                              |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1 — Omen**       | An obvious magical sign, brief affliction, unwanted attention or minor demand.           |
| **2 — Price**      | Stress, injury, damaged equipment, a binding task or immediate danger.                   |
| **3 — Breach**     | A lasting mark, terrible sacrifice, severe transformation or manifestation of the Other. |
| **4+ — Incursion** | The Other reaches directly into the world and permanently changes the situation.         |

## Witch spells

Witchcraft is personal. It works through names, tokens, sympathy, hospitality and old obligations. A Witch may know an Other as a patron, ancestor, lover, jailer or god.

### Witch-Sight

_Requirement: Witch, Magical_

**Cost:** 1–3 Focus

For the rest of the scene, you perceive signs left by the Others, their servants and their magic. When you closely examine a person, creature, object or place, the GM tells you whether an unnatural influence is present and gives one concrete sensory clue to its nature.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Ask what the influence did most recently.
- Ask what the influence presently wants.
- See through one magical concealment in your zone.
- Learn the direction to the influence's origin if it is nearby.

### Hex

_Requirement: Witch, Magical_

**Cost:** 1–3 Focus

Name a creature you can see and test _Presence + Will_ against its _Will + Will_. On a Success, its next test becomes one step harder.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Affect one additional test (**repeatable**).
- Affect another creature in the same zone. It resists separately (**repeatable**).
- Make one affected test two steps harder instead of one.

### Familiar

_Requirement: Witch, Magical_

When you learn this spell, choose a small animal as the shape of the familiar granted by your Other.

**Cost:** 1–3 Focus

For the rest of the scene, you can give your familiar silent instructions and see and hear through it while it remains within three zones. It can perform anything an ordinary animal of its shape could manage. If it is badly harmed, it vanishes and cannot return until after your next rest.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Increase the range to roughly one mile.
- Speak through the familiar in your own voice.
- Let the familiar converse with you intelligently.
- Deliver one touch-range spell through the familiar before this spell ends. The delivered spell has its own Focus cost and Price Dice.

### Borrowed Shape

_Requirement: Witch, Magical_

**Cost:** 2–4 Focus

Take the form of a common animal no larger than a wolf until the end of the scene. Your equipment falls where you stood. You retain your Attributes and mind, but can only perform actions the animal's body permits. You cannot speak, use equipment or activate other Abilities. Becoming Wounded immediately ends the spell.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Your equipment merges into the shape and returns with you.
- Take the form of an animal as large as a bear or horse.
- Retain the ability to speak.
- Remain transformed until the next sunrise.

### Speak with the Unquiet

_Requirement: Witch, Magical_

**Cost:** 1–3 Focus

Touch a corpse or recognisable set of remains. The dead person briefly speaks and answers one question. They know only what they knew in life, recognise neither later events nor hidden truths and retain their former loyalties and prejudices.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Ask one additional question (**repeatable**).
- Compel the dead to answer plainly and truthfully.
- Work through a fragment of the body or a treasured possession rather than the remains.
- Let everyone in your zone hear and understand the dead.

### Dream Sending

_Requirement: Witch, Magical_

**Cost:** 1–3 Focus

Spend ten minutes in a trance while holding something once touched by a person known to you. The next time that person sleeps, they receive a brief dream-message from you. Its imagery bears the unmistakable Sign of your Other.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Receive a brief answer when the target wakes.
- Enter the dream and converse until either participant chooses to leave.
- Ensure the target remembers the message perfectly.
- Reach the target during a brief waking vision instead of waiting for sleep.

### Bind the Shadow

_Requirement: Witch, Magical_

**Cost:** 1–3 Focus

Pin a visible creature's shadow to the ground and test _Lore + Will_ against its _Agility + Will_. On a Success, the creature cannot leave its present position until the end of its next turn, though it can act normally.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Extend the binding by one round (**repeatable**).
- Make the target's actions one step harder while bound.
- Pull the target prone when the binding takes hold.
- Prevent the target from speaking above a whisper while bound.

### Call the Pale Mist

_Requirement: Witch, Magical_

**Cost:** 1–3 Focus

Unnatural mist fills your zone until the end of your next turn. Anything beyond arm's reach is concealed, and attacks made through the mist are one step harder.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Fill one adjacent zone as well (**repeatable**).
- Allow yourself and a number of named allies up to your Will to see through the mist.
- Extend the duration until the end of the scene.
- Make the mist follow you as you move.

## Alchemist spells

Alchemical magic is embodied in salts, metals, vapours, preserved organs and unstable compounds. Alchemists often claim that their formulae command natural forces, but every successful working still opens a path to an Other.

An Alchemist needs an alchemical kit and suitable supplies when preparing these spells. The character is assumed to prepare ordinary vessels and doses between scenes. If deprived of the kit, already prepared materials last until the end of the next scene; after that, Alchemist spells cannot be activated until the kit is recovered or replaced.

### Bottled Flame

_Requirement: Alchemist, Magical_

**Cost:** 1–3 Focus

Throw a sealed vessel at a target in your zone or an adjacent zone and test _Agility + Lore_ as a ranged attack. It inflicts Damage 2, uses the [[Weapons and Critical Hits#Shot and blast criticals|Shot and blast]] Critical table and ignites exposed flammable material.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Add +1 Damage (**repeatable**).
- Strike one additional target close to the first. Use the same attack result.
- The flame clings to the target and inflicts Damage 1 at the end of its next turn unless extinguished.
- Leave the affected area burning until the end of the scene.

### Smoke of Revelation

_Requirement: Alchemist, Magical_

**Cost:** 1–3 Focus

Break a vessel that fills your zone with thin, luminous smoke until the end of the scene. Invisible creatures, magical writing, concealed passages and effects created by the Others disturb the smoke and become perceptible to anyone present.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Fill one adjacent zone as well (**repeatable**).
- Reveal the true appearance beneath magical disguises.
- Make the smoke cling to one revealed creature after it leaves the area.
- Prevent one revealed effect from becoming concealed again during the scene.

### Circle of Salt and Iron

_Requirement: Alchemist, Magical_

**Cost:** 1–3 Focus

Use an action to draw a line across a doorway or a circle around a small position. Test _Lore + Will_ and record the result. Until the end of the scene, a manifested servant of an Other must equal or exceed your Successes with _Will + Will_ to cross the boundary. It can try once per turn.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Ward one additional doorway or connected boundary.
- Extend the duration until sunrise.
- Inflict Damage 2, ignoring mundane Protection, when an affected creature forces its way across.
- Prevent an affected creature from targeting anything beyond the boundary with magic.

### Quickening Draught

_Requirement: Alchemist, Magical_

**Cost:** 1–3 Focus

You or a willing creature beside you drinks a prepared draught. The drinker gains +3 dice on their next _Agility_ or _Might_ test during this scene.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Apply the bonus to one additional test (**repeatable**).
- Let the drinker immediately move within their zone without using an action.
- Suppress one penalty caused by exhaustion, pain or being Wounded until the end of the scene.
- Affect one additional willing drinker beside you.

### Mercurial Transmutation

_Requirement: Alchemist, Magical_

**Cost:** 1–3 Focus

Touch a nonliving, simple object no larger than a chest and change one physical property until the end of the scene. It can become hard, soft, brittle, flexible, transparent or opaque. This cannot create a complex mechanism or a valuable substance.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Increase the affected amount from a chest to a cart, or from a cart to a large fixture (**repeatable**).
- Change the material into another common substance as well as changing its properties.
- Extend the duration until sunrise.
- Shape the affected material into one simple form without tools.

### Homunculus

_Requirement: Alchemist, Magical_

**Cost:** 2–4 Focus

Spend ten minutes assembling a small servant from clay, wire, preserved tissue and a drop of your blood. It obeys simple instructions until the end of the scene. It can carry small objects, manipulate simple devices and enter places a cat could reach. It rolls four dice when an uncertain action is required and cannot fight effectively.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Give it +2 dice on all tests (**repeatable**).
- Allow it to speak, reason and report what it observes.
- Make it strong enough to drag a person or operate heavy mechanisms.
- Extend its existence until the next sunrise.

### Elemental Vessel

_Requirement: Alchemist, Magical_

**Cost:** 2–4 Focus

Present a specially prepared vessel to a natural flame, rushing water, violent wind or similar elemental phenomenon no larger than a person. Test _Lore + Will_. On a Success, the phenomenon is drawn into the vessel and the immediate hazard ends. Breaking the vessel later uses an action and releases what was captured. The vessel loses its power at the end of the scene.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Capture a phenomenon filling an entire zone.
- Preserve the vessel until the next sunrise.
- Shape the released element so that it avoids chosen creatures or objects.
- Bind a minor manifested elemental servant instead. It resists with _Will + Will_.

### Panacea of Borrowed Breath

_Requirement: Alchemist, Magical_

**Cost:** 2–4 Focus

Spend one minute administering a prepared dose and test _Agility + Lore_. On a Success, stop the patient's bleeding and move them one step towards Stable on the dying track. Alternatively, stop a poison or disease from worsening until the next sunrise.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Move the patient one additional step towards Stable (**repeatable**).
- Remove one condition caused by poison or disease.
- Restore consciousness if the patient's injuries permit it.
- Suppress the difficulty increase from being Wounded until the end of the scene.

The Panacea does not remove accumulated damage, critical injuries or permanent effects.

## Vicar spells

Vicar spells take the form of prayers, blessings, prohibitions and sacraments. The established faith may call the answering power a god, angel or saint; this does not mean that its theologians understand the Other behind the name.

Unless a spell says otherwise, the Vicar must be able to speak and present a holy symbol or other token of office.

### Rebuke the Unclean

_Requirement: Vicar, Magical_

**Cost:** 1–3 Focus

Present your holy symbol and test _Presence + Will_ against the _Will + Will_ of a manifested servant of an Other in your zone. On a Success, it cannot willingly approach you until the end of its next turn.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Extend the prohibition by one round (**repeatable**).
- Drive the creature into an adjacent zone.
- Affect one additional creature in your zone. It resists separately (**repeatable**).
- Prevent the creature from targeting you with magic while rebuked.

### Sanctuary

_Requirement: Vicar, Magical_

**Cost:** 1–3 Focus

Touch a willing creature. Until the end of its next turn, anyone attempting to attack it directly must first win a _Will + Will_ test against your _Presence + Will_. Indirect dangers and attacks already in motion are unaffected. Sanctuary ends for the protected creature if it attacks or deliberately harms someone.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Extend the protection by one round (**repeatable**).
- Protect one additional willing creature beside you (**repeatable**).
- Prevent hostile magic from directly targeting the protected creature as well.
- Allow the protected creature to move through one occupied position without being stopped.

### Stay the Passing

_Requirement: Vicar, Magical_

**Cost:** 1–3 Focus

Touch a Dying character and test _Presence + Will_. On a Success, stop their bleeding and move them one step towards Stable on the dying track.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Move the character one additional step towards Stable (**repeatable**).
- Restore consciousness if their injuries permit it.
- Prevent them from making survival tests until the next sunrise.
- Suppress the difficulty increase from being Wounded until the end of the scene.

This spell does not remove accumulated damage, critical injuries or permanent effects.

### Litany Against Fear

_Requirement: Vicar, Magical_

**Cost:** 1–3 Focus

Use your action to address allies who can hear you. Choose one ally in your zone. They may immediately repeat a failed test against fear, supernatural influence or mental control.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Affect one additional ally who can hear you (**repeatable**).
- Remove 1 Stress from one affected ally (**repeatable**).
- Make one affected ally immune to fear until the end of the scene.
- Extend the Litany into one adjacent zone.

### Consecrate Ground

_Requirement: Vicar, Magical_

**Cost:** 2–4 Focus

Spend ten minutes marking and blessing one zone. Until the end of the scene, manifested beings allied to Others other than your patron must win a _Will + Will_ test against your _Presence + Will_ before entering. A creature already present must pass the same test to willingly harm anyone inside.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Consecrate one connected zone as well (**repeatable**).
- Extend the duration until the next sunrise.
- Prevent hostile spells from crossing the boundary unless their caster wins the same test.
- Exclude a named mortal servant of another Other as though they were a manifested being.

### Exorcism

_Requirement: Vicar, Magical_

**Cost:** 2–4 Focus

Spend ten uninterrupted minutes confronting a possessing or inhabiting entity. Test _Presence + Will_ against its _Will + Will_. On a Success, it is expelled from the creature, object or place it inhabits and cannot possess the same subject again before the end of the scene.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Prevent the entity from returning until the next sunrise.
- Force it to reveal one name or title by which it can be summoned.
- End one curse or supernatural condition it placed on the subject.
- Immediately subject the expelled entity to Rebuke the Unclean without spending additional Focus.

### Revelation

_Requirement: Vicar, Magical_

**Cost:** 2–4 Focus

Spend ten minutes in prayer and ask one question about a person, place or course of action. The GM provides a truthful but symbolic vision from the perspective of your Other. The Other reveals what it perceives and considers significant, which may not be what a human would consider important.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Ask one additional question (**repeatable**).
- Receive one answer in clear words rather than symbols.
- See one likely consequence within the next day.
- Learn what offering, prohibition or action would win the Other's further aid.

### Oath Under Witness

_Requirement: Vicar, Magical_

**Cost:** 1–3 Focus

Two or more willing creatures speak an oath before you and touch your holy symbol. Until the next sunrise, anyone who knowingly breaks the oath gains a visible supernatural mark and takes 2 Stress. The Other immediately knows that the oath was broken.

For each Power level, choose one:

- Extend the oath's duration from sunrise to one week, from one week to one season, or from one season until its terms are fulfilled (**repeatable**).
- Know immediately which participant broke the oath.
- Know the direction to the oathbreaker until the mark fades.
- Grant each participant +3 dice on one test made to uphold the oath.
