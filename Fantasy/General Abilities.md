# General Abilities

**General Abilities** are available to characters of every Profession and Ancestry. They represent hard-earned tricks, habits and training rather than a particular trade.

Each Ability is learned once and has no ranks. A new character can choose one General Ability instead of an [[Ancestries|Ancestry Ability]]. Further General Abilities can be learned through advancement.

Unless an Ability says otherwise, it can be activated whenever its trigger occurs. Bonuses from Abilities use the ordinary rules and their dice can generate Focus.

## Combat abilities

### Battle Ready

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Activate when combat begins. Ready a weapon, move to any position within your zone and add +3 to your initiative result. You can use Battle Ready only once per combat.

### Brutal Blow

**Cost:** 1 Focus

After hitting with a melee attack, add +2 Damage. Until the beginning of your next turn, attacks against you are one step easier.

### Careful Shot

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Before making a ranged attack, gain +3 dice. You must have remained still since the beginning of your turn, and you cannot move during the same turn.

### Counterstroke

**Cost:** 1 Focus

When a creature beside you misses you with a melee attack, immediately make a melee attack against it. The attack is one step harder and does not use your next action.

### Hold the Line

**Cost:** 1 Focus

When a creature moves within reach of your readied melee weapon, test _Combat + Might_ against its _Agility + Might_. On a Success, its movement ends beside you. If you have a Reach weapon, you may also inflict that weapon's normal Damage without adding Effect.

### Mounted Fighter

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Activate at the start of your turn while mounted. Until the end of the turn, ignore one difficulty increase caused by riding or fighting from horseback. Your mount may also move within its current zone without using your action.

### Skirmisher

**Cost:** 1 Focus

After making a melee attack, move to another position within your zone without using an action. Unless an enemy has blocked the route, it cannot prevent this movement.

### Stand Between

**Cost:** 1 Focus

When a creature beside you is targeted by an attack, become the target instead. Gain +1 Protection against that attack. You must be aware of the attack and able to reach the original target.

## Travel and survival abilities

### Campcraft

**Cost:** 1 Focus

After spending at least half an hour establishing camp, choose one benefit:

- The camp cannot be found without an active search.
- Everyone sheltering there ignores one penalty caused by ordinary weather.
- One person who rests there removes 1 additional Stress.

Campcraft can be used once per rest.

### Delver

**Cost:** 1 Focus

After examining part of a ruin, cave, mine or sewer, ask the GM one question: what is structurally dangerous, where air or water is moving, what passed through recently or which visible route leads deeper? The answer is truthful and based on physical evidence.

### Forager

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Before testing to find food, water or fuel in the wild, gain +3 dice. On a Success, find enough for yourself and one additional person per Effect. Nothing can be found where the required resource does not exist.

### Pathfinder

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Before a group tests to navigate or cross difficult country, make the test one step easier. On a Success, also choose one:

- Reduce the journey's travel time.
- Avoid one ordinary environmental hazard.
- Leave no obvious trail for casual pursuers.

Pathfinder can benefit a given journey only once per day.

### Scrounger

**Cost:** 1 Focus

After searching an abandoned camp, battlefield, ruin or refuse heap, find one common and useful item that could reasonably be present. Examples include cord, lamp oil, a waterskin, scrap metal, a blanket or a few pieces of ammunition. The GM may say that the item is damaged, incomplete or carries an unwanted clue.

### Surefooted

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Before testing to climb, balance, jump or cross dangerous ground, gain +3 dice. If you succeed, you may also help one nearby character across without requiring a separate test from them.

### Vigilant

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Activate when an ambush, hidden threat or sudden environmental danger is revealed. You are not surprised and may immediately warn everyone who can hear you. Allies who heed the warning gain +2 dice on their first test to avoid the danger.

### Weather Eye

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Spend a few minutes studying the sky, wind and local signs. The GM tells you what ordinary weather to expect during the next day and where the nearest plausible shelter lies. If the weather is supernatural, you instead learn that something is wrong and receive one sensory clue.

## Social and practical abilities

### Appraiser

**Cost:** 1 Focus

After examining an object, learn its approximate value and whether its workmanship, materials or age are being misrepresented. On a successful _Awareness + Lore_ test, also identify one hidden flaw, unusual property or clue to its origin.

### Field Dressing

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Before testing to stop [[Weapons and Critical Hits#Critical conditions|Bleeding or Severe Bleeding]], or to stabilise a Dying character, gain +3 dice. Treatment still requires the normal time and equipment.

### Imposing

**Cost:** 1 Focus

After you display force, inflict an injury or make a credible threat, test _Might + Presence_ against the target's _Presence + Will_. On a Success, an ordinary opponent hesitates, backs away or reveals what would make them surrender. This cannot force someone to accept certain death.

### Jury-Rig

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Spend one minute working on a broken mundane object with whatever tools are available. It functions until the end of the scene. Complex machinery or damage requiring replacement parts also requires an _Agility + Lore_ test.

### Packwise

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Produce one small, common item that you could reasonably have packed earlier, such as chalk, a candle, twine, a whetstone, a cup or a handful of nails. The item cannot be a weapon, specialised tool, valuable object or additional supply of an exhausted resource. Use Packwise only once per scene.

### Read the Room

**Cost:** 1 Focus

After observing a conversation or gathering for a few minutes, ask the GM one question: who holds real authority, who most wants violence, who is being excluded or who is hiding the strongest emotion? The answer reflects what can be learned from behaviour, not hidden thoughts.

### Silver Tongue

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Before testing to persuade, bargain or deceive, gain +3 dice if you offer the other person something they plausibly want. On a Success, they also reveal one concern or condition that could change their decision later.

### Well Connected

**Cost:** 1 Focus

When you enter a settlement, name a trade, institution or social circle. You know someone locally connected to it. The GM decides who they are, what history you share and what favour, payment or risk accompanies their help. Use Well Connected only once per settlement.
