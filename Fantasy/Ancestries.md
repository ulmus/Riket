# Ancestries

An **Ancestry** describes a people whose inherited nature differs from ordinary humanity. It does not determine culture, morality or Profession.

Each Ancestry provides:

- One raised Attribute cap.
- One Base Ability possessed by every member of that Ancestry.
- Access to optional Ancestry Abilities during character creation and advancement.

Ancestry does not add or subtract Attribute points. Every Attribute is limited to 4 during character creation. A raised cap allows the listed Attribute to reach 5 through later advancement. Ancestry and Profession cap increases do not stack beyond 5.

Base Abilities are automatic and do not count against the character's chosen Abilities. A new character also chooses one Ability from their Profession and one [[General Abilities|General Ability]] or optional Ability from their Ancestry.

Ancestry Abilities are innate rather than Magical. They do not roll Price Dice unless an Ability is explicitly marked as Magical. Some traditions claim that the nonhuman peoples were changed by the Others in ages past, but inherited traits do not constitute a pact.

## Human

Humans are the most numerous and varied people in the known lands. They share no supernatural inheritance and have no single ancestral culture. Their strength lies in what an individual is willing to risk when training and good sense are no longer enough.

Human is the **non-ancestry**. A Human receives no raised Attribute cap and has no exclusive Ancestry Abilities. Their Profession can still raise one Attribute cap to 5.

### Determination

After making an Attribute test, spend any amount of Focus to add the same number of d12s to the result. Determination can be used only once per test.

Each added die showing 10+ adds a Success. Each 12 also generates Focus as normal. If an added die shows 1, the GM introduces a Complication. Additional 1s increase its severity.

## Elf

**Attribute cap:** Awareness 5

Elves are long-lived but not immortal. Their oldest settlements stand in forests, marshes and abandoned districts where roads refuse to remain straight. Human theologians claim that their ancestors served the Others. Elven accounts contradict one another and often predate the churches making the accusation.

Elves tend to notice changes in light, sound and season before others. This does not make them wiser. It gives them more time to realise that something is wrong.

### Elf-Sight

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Activate before an _Awareness_ test. You can see clearly in dim light and gain +3 dice when detecting something hidden, distant or unnaturally concealed. Elf-Sight does not reveal the nature of magic by itself.

### Forest Step

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Move through dense vegetation, mud, snow or similar natural terrain without slowing down until the end of your turn. You make no unnecessary sound and leave no trail that can be followed without an active search.

### Long Memory

**Cost:** 1 Focus

After examining an old object, ruin, family name or work of art, ask the GM one question: who made it, what purpose it served, what important event it witnessed or what has changed since its creation? The answer reflects elven memory and tradition, not supernatural omniscience.

### Perfect Stillness

**Cost:** 1 Focus

While motionless among natural cover, become hidden without a test. Anyone searching for you must win a test against your _Agility + Awareness_. The effect ends when you move, speak above a whisper or take an obvious action.

### Graceful Escape

**Cost:** 1 Focus

When you are grabbed, bound or physically trapped, escape without a test if an ordinary person could conceivably slip free. If escape remains uncertain, gain +3 dice on the test and move to another position within your zone on a Success.

## Dwarf

**Attribute cap:** Might 5

Dwarves built their holds beneath mountain passes and rich seams of ore. Their clans preserve contracts, debts and technical secrets across generations. In the cities they are found in foundries, artillery companies, guild halls and counting houses as often as underground.

Stories say the first Dwarves were shaped to withstand the weight beneath the world. Dwarven records describe this as metaphor, theology or an unpaid debt, depending on which archive is consulted.

### Unyielding

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Activate when a bodily or mental condition affects you. Ignore one penalty caused by pain, fear, poison, exhaustion or another condition until the end of your next turn. Unyielding does not remove marked Damage, prevent Dying or negate a permanent injury.

### Stonewise

**Cost:** 1 Focus

After touching worked stone, masonry or exposed rock, ask the GM one question: what is unstable, what is hidden behind it, where it came from or what kind of tools shaped it? The answer is truthful if physical evidence could reveal it.

### Forgewise

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Before testing to make or repair an object primarily made from metal or stone, gain +3 dice. On a Success, complete the work in half the usual time or remove one flaw that inferior tools or materials would otherwise cause.

### Burden Bearer

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Before testing to lift, drag, hold or break something through strength, gain +3 dice. Until the end of the scene, you can also carry an injured person or another heavy burden without it making your movement harder.

### Oath-Bound

**Cost:** 1 Focus

After freely swearing a concrete oath, activate before a test that directly fulfils it to gain +3 dice. If you knowingly abandon or betray the oath, you cannot use Oath-Bound again until you make restitution to the wronged party or your clan.

## Halfling

**Attribute cap:** Cunning 5

Halflings are a small people from river valleys, hedged farmland and travelling communities. Larger neighbours often treat them as harmless dependants, welcome servants or convenient scapegoats. Halflings have survived by understanding exactly what others expect to see.

Their settlements value shared meals, private agreements and news carried between households. A Halfling may leave home for decades and still discover that an aunt has preserved their place at the table along with every debt they incurred abroad.

### Fortune's Child

**Cost:** 1 Focus

After making an Attribute test, reroll up to two of its dice. You must keep the new results. Any new 12 generates Focus as normal.

### Overlooked

**Cost:** 1 Focus

When you are among a crowd, servants, prisoners or people who consider you unimportant, become unnoticed without a test. Others can still find you through an active search. The effect ends when you draw attention to yourself or openly threaten someone.

### Small Enough

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Slip through a narrow opening, out of loose bonds or into a hiding place without a test when your size makes it reasonably possible. If the attempt remains uncertain or dangerous, gain +3 dice on the test instead.

### Hearthkeeper

**Cost:** 1 Focus

After sharing a meal and at least an hour of safe rest, choose a number of participants up to your Presence. Each removes 1 Stress. A character can benefit from Hearthkeeper only once per rest.

### Unexpected Courage

**Cost:** 1 Focus

After failing a test against fear or intimidation, turn the failure into a Success with no Effect. One ally who can see you gains +2 dice on their next test against the same source of fear.
