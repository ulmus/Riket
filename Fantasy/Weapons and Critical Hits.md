# Weapons and Critical Hits

## Attacks and damage

Use the following Attribute pairs for ordinary weapon attacks:

- **Melee:** _Combat + Might_.
- **Finesse melee:** _Agility + Combat_.
- **Ranged:** _Awareness + Combat_.
- **Thrown:** _Agility + Combat_.

On a hit, mark Damage equal to:

**the attack's Effect + the weapon's Damage − the target's Protection**

Damage cannot be reduced below 0. Mark the character's Damage boxes from left to right. When Damage reaches a new row, the character suffers that row's condition.

- **Fit:** No penalty.
- **Wounded:** All actions are one step harder.
- **Incapacitated:** You fall unconscious and cannot act.
- **Dying:** You become Dying and begin making survival tests.

Each row contains a number of usable boxes equal to the character's Resilience. Continue onto the next row when those boxes are filled. Further Damage after the Dying row is filled causes immediate death.

## Critical hits

When at least one attack die shows **12**, the attacker gains Focus as normal. The attack also causes a **Critical Hit** if it hits and inflicts at least 1 Damage after Protection.

Roll 1d12 on the table matching the weapon's Critical type and apply these modifiers:

- **+1** for each additional attack die showing 12.
- **+2** if the weapon is Penetrating.
- **−2** against a particularly large or resilient creature, at the GM's discretion.

Roll only once, even if several attack dice showed 12. Results above 12 are possible.

If the rolled description does not fit the attack, the GM changes the fictional injury while retaining its mechanical consequences.

### Critical conditions

- **Dazed:** Your next action is one step harder.
- **Bleeding:** Mark 1 Damage after every scene in which you exert yourself. Treatment takes one minute and a successful _Agility + Lore_ test.
- **Severe Bleeding:** Mark 1 Damage at the end of every round, or once per minute outside combat. A character beside you can spend their action holding pressure to pause the bleeding. Stopping it requires one minute and a successful _Agility + Lore_ test.
- **Incapacitated:** You cannot take actions. Successful treatment or sufficient recovery may restore consciousness, but it does not remove marked Damage or the injury.
- **Dying:** Use the normal dying and survival rules. Becoming Dying again worsens the character's condition by one step.

Treatment stops bleeding or stabilises the immediate injury. It does not remove marked Damage, restore a destroyed limb or erase a permanent effect.

## Blunt criticals

Use this table for fists, clubs, maces, hammers, falls and other crushing impacts.

|      1d12 | Result                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------: | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|   **1–3** | **Staggered.** You fall prone or drop one held object, attacker's choice. Take 1 Stress.                                                                             |
|   **4–6** | **Concussion.** You are Dazed. Awareness and Lore tests are one step harder until the end of the scene.                                                              |
|   **7–9** | **Broken bone.** Roll 1d12: 1–3 left arm, 4–6 right arm, 7–9 left leg, 10–12 right leg. The limb is unusable until treated and remains impaired while healing.       |
| **10–11** | **Crushed ribs or skull.** You are Incapacitated. Physical actions are one step harder after you regain consciousness until the injury receives treatment.           |
|    **12** | **Massive internal injury.** You are Dying. Treatment is one step harder. If you survive, one affected Attribute is reduced by 1 until a long recovery is completed. |
|   **13+** | **Fatal trauma.** Your skull, neck or chest is crushed. You die immediately.                                                                                         |

|   **4–6** | **Deep cut.** You are Bleeding. Tests involving the injured area are one step harder until treated.                                         |
|   **7–9** | **Ruined limb.** Roll 1d12: 1–3 left arm, 4–6 right arm, 7–9 left leg, 10–12 right leg. The limb is unusable and you are Severely Bleeding. |

## Shot and blast criticals

Use this table for black-powder weapons, explosions, fire, acid and violent magical attacks.

When this table is used for fire or acid, Bleeding represents ongoing burning or corrosion. The treatment test extinguishes or neutralises it.

|      1d12 | Result                                                                                                                                                 |
| --------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
|   **1–3** | **Grazed or scorched.** You are Dazed and take 1 Stress.                                                                                               |
|   **4–6** | **Shot or burned through.** You are Bleeding. Physical actions are one step harder until treated.                                                      |
|   **7–9** | **Shattered limb.** Roll 1d12: 1–3 left arm, 4–6 right arm, 7–9 left leg, 10–12 right leg. The limb is unusable and you are Severely Bleeding.         |
| **10–11** | **Organ trauma.** A lung, the belly or several ribs are destroyed. You are Incapacitated and Severely Bleeding. Treatment is one step harder.          |
|    **12** | **Vital hit.** You are Dying. Treatment is one step harder. If you survive, you suffer a permanent scar, impairment or loss appropriate to the injury. |
|   **13+** | **Catastrophic hit.** The head, heart or most of the torso is destroyed. You die immediately.                                                          |

## Weapon qualities

### Area

Choose a small position within range. Make one attack test and compare it with the difficulty for every exposed creature there. Calculate Effect and Damage separately for each target.

### Defensive

Gain +2 dice when actively parrying with this weapon.

### Finesse

You may use _Agility + Combat_ instead of _Combat + Might_ for melee attacks with this weapon.

### Loud

The weapon can be heard throughout the location. Black-powder smoke and muzzle flash reveal the attacker's position.

### Penetrating

Add +2 to any Critical Hit roll caused by this weapon.

### Reach

The weapon can strike from several paces away. A creature with a shorter weapon must close before attacking. Once an enemy has closed inside its reach, attacks with the weapon become one step harder.

### Reload X

The weapon requires the listed number of actions to reload after every shot. Reloading requires ammunition and both hands. A character can interrupt the process and continue later.

### Scatter

Add +1 Damage against a target at Near range. The weapon cannot attack beyond Short range.

### Thrown

The weapon can be thrown to Short range using _Agility + Combat_. It must be recovered before it can be used again unless it was consumed or destroyed by the attack.

### Two-handed

The weapon requires two hands to attack or reload.

### Unreliable

If an attack fails and at least one attack die shows 1, a firearm misfires or an explosive fuse behaves unpredictably. Clearing a firearm requires one action. The GM decides whether a failed explosive can be safely recovered.
