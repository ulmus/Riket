# Professions

A **Profession** describes training, experience and a place in the world. It has two mechanical effects:

- When an action clearly falls within the Profession, the test becomes one step easier.
- The Profession raises the cap of its listed Attribute from 4 to 5.

A Profession applies to its tradecraft, contacts and specialist knowledge. It does not normally make direct attack or defence tests easier. Those benefits come from Abilities.

A new character chooses one Ability from their Profession. Alchemists, Vicars and Witches may choose a spell available to their Profession instead. Every character can also choose from [[General Abilities]]. Further Abilities can be learned through advancement.

## Alchemist

**Attribute cap:** Lore 5

Alchemists understand compounds, acids, medicines, metals, glassmaking, distillation and black powder. Their Profession applies when identifying an unknown substance, preparing a known formula, treating poison, assessing an explosion or working in a laboratory. It does not replace a trained surgeon when treating wounds.

**Typical equipment:** Alchemical kit, protective apron, mortar and pestle, vials, scales, notebook, knife.

**Spell access:** [[Spells#Alchemist spells|Alchemist spells]].

### The Necessary Mixture

**Cost:** 1 Focus

If you have your alchemical kit, produce one common preparation you could reasonably have made in advance: acid, glue, smoke powder, solvent, stimulant, sedative or another mundane compound approved by the GM. It lasts until used and is lost at the end of the scene.

### Flash Powder

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Throw a prepared charge into your zone or an adjacent zone. Everyone looking toward it tests _Agility + Awareness_. On a failure, they are **Dazed** until the end of their next turn: all their actions are one step harder.

### Field Antidote

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Touch a poisoned character and test _Agility + Lore_. On a Success, the poison stops worsening for the rest of the scene. Each Effect can also remove one condition caused by the poison. This does not repair damage already suffered.

### Volatile Charge

**Cost:** 2 Focus

Spend one minute preparing a charge from your kit. It can breach a barred door, destroy a small mechanism or attack everyone in one zone with Damage 4 using the [[Weapons and Critical Hits#Shot and blast criticals|Shot and blast]] Critical table. The blast is heard far away, fills the area with smoke and can start a fire. The charge becomes useless if it is not employed during the scene.

## Huntsman

**Attribute cap:** Awareness 5

Huntsmen understand tracks, animals, weather, terrain, hunting weapons and life beyond cultivated land. Their Profession applies when following spoor, finding food and shelter, moving through wilderness, judging an animal or predicting weather. It does not make ordinary ranged attacks easier.

**Typical equipment:** Bow or hunting gun, long knife, cloak, cord, snares, firemaking kit, field bag.

### Mark the Quarry

**Cost:** 1 Focus

After examining a trail, wound, discarded object or other sign, name the creature that left it as your quarry. Until the end of the scene, you always know whether you are moving closer to or farther from it. Mundane attempts by the quarry to hide its trail do not stop you.

### Patient Shot

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Before making a ranged attack, ignore one circumstance that would make the test harder, such as cover, poor light, wind or long range. You must have remained still since the start of your turn.

### Set the Snare

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Spend one minute preparing a trap in your zone. The first creature to cross it tests _Agility + Awareness_. On a failure, it is caught and cannot leave the zone until it spends an action freeing itself. A dangerous snare also inflicts Damage 2 if suitable materials were available.

### Beastwise

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Calm an ordinary animal or make it perform one familiar action without a test. Against a trained, hostile or unnatural beast, gain +3 dice on one test to approach, direct or understand it.

## Mercenary

**Attribute cap:** Combat 5

Mercenaries understand military discipline, weapons, armour, formations, camps, sieges and the customs of soldiers. Their Profession applies when assessing a battlefield, maintaining military equipment, recognising a unit, organising a camp or dealing with other professional soldiers. It does not make ordinary attack tests easier.

**Typical equipment:** Chosen military weapon, sidearm, gambeson or breastplate, helmet, bedroll, whetstone.

### Guard

**Cost:** 1 Focus

When you are attacked in close combat, defend actively even though it is not your turn. Test _Agility + Combat_ and subtract the test's Effect from the attacker's Successes.

### Hold Fast

**Cost:** 1 Focus

When pain, fear or a condition would make your actions harder, ignore one such penalty until the end of your next turn. You still suffer the underlying wound, Stress or condition.

### Press the Advantage

**Cost:** 1 Focus

After hitting in close combat, force the target back into an adjacent position, knock it prone or disarm it. The attack still inflicts its normal damage. A much larger target can resist with _Might + Might_ against your _Combat + Might_.

### Powder Drill

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Reload a black-powder weapon or clear its misfire as part of the same action used to fire it. You must have powder, shot and both hands available.

## Thief

**Attribute cap:** Cunning 5

Thieves understand locks, traps, burglary, surveillance, hiding places, stolen goods and criminal contacts. Their Profession applies when breaking into a guarded place, opening locks, detecting mundane traps, casing a target or navigating the criminal world.

**Typical equipment:** Lock picks, dark cloak, soft boots, hooded lantern, chalk, knife, coil of thin rope.

### Open Anything

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Before testing to open a lock or disable a mundane trap, gain +3 dice. On a Success, the work leaves no visible sign of tampering.

### Light Fingers

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Take or plant a hand-sized object without a test if nobody is specifically watching your hands. Under direct observation, gain +3 dice on the _Agility + Cunning_ test instead.

### Vanish

**Cost:** 1 Focus

When you reach cover, darkness or a distracted crowd, become hidden without spending an action. Enemies must actively search and win a test against your _Agility + Cunning_ before they can target you directly.

### Knife in the Dark

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Before attacking a target that is unaware of you, add +2 Damage. If the attack causes a critical wound, add +1 to its critical result.

## Vicar

**Attribute cap:** Presence 5

Vicars understand doctrine, literacy, local law, burial customs, pastoral care and the workings of the established faith. Their Profession applies when interpreting scripture, conducting a recognised rite, counselling the troubled, dealing with religious authorities or identifying signs of heresy. A Vicar need not possess magic.

**Typical equipment:** Holy symbol, vestments, travelling clothes, scripture, writing case, lantern, walking staff.

**Spell access:** [[Spells#Vicar spells|Vicar spells]]. A Vicar who learns a spell has entered a pact, whether or not they recognise it as such.

### Moral Authority

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Invoke your office when asking for an audience, temporary shelter or another reasonable courtesy. Ordinary members of your faith grant it unless doing so would place them in immediate danger. Powerful or hostile people can demand a _Presence + Will_ test.

### Rallying Homily

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Use your action to address allies who can hear you. Each removes 1 Stress. A character can benefit from Rallying Homily only once per scene.

### Last Rites

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Remain beside a Dying character and perform the proper rite. The character makes no survival tests for one hour, but remains Dying and still requires treatment. The effect ends if you abandon the rite before another person takes over their care.

### Confessor's Ear

**Cost:** 1 Focus

After listening to someone speak privately for at least ten minutes, ask the GM which emotion, desire or fear the speaker is trying hardest to conceal. If the speaker came to you voluntarily for counsel or confession, also learn one useful truth they revealed indirectly.

## Witch

**Attribute cap:** Will 5

Witches understand omens, herbs, curses, spirits, old bargains and customs that predate the established faith. Their Profession applies when recognising supernatural influence, preparing folk remedies, interpreting an omen, negotiating with a minor spirit or recalling a local taboo. It does not automatically make spell attacks easier.

**Typical equipment:** Knife, charm bag, herbs, thread, chalk, small mirror, tokens from past bargains.

**Spell access:** [[Spells#Witch spells|Witch spells]]. A Witch who chooses a spell defines their pact when they learn the first one.

### Herb-Wise

**Cost:** 1 Focus

When treating poison, disease or an injury with gathered herbs, gain +3 dice on the test. On a Success, the patient also ignores one penalty caused by pain or sickness until the end of the scene. This does not remove damage or a critical injury.

### Read the Signs

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Spend a minute examining tracks, weather, animal behaviour or some other natural omen. Ask the GM one question: what recently passed this way, what immediate danger is approaching or what local taboo has been broken? The answer is truthful but limited to what the signs could reveal.

### Old Hospitality

**Cost:** 1 Focus

Invoke an old custom when seeking food, shelter or local news in an isolated household or pagan community. The inhabitants grant simple hospitality unless doing so presents an immediate danger. They may expect you to observe their customs or repay the courtesy later.

### Keeper of Names

**Cost:** 1 Focus

When you encounter a local Other, supernatural servant, curse or haunted place, recall one relevant name, taboo, traditional offering or mundane ward. The GM tells you one true thing that can alter how it is approached. This knowledge does not compel the being or break the curse by itself.
